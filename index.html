ታሪካችን · The Ceremony

The buna ceremony, kept whole.

In Ethiopia, coffee is never rushed. Green beans are roasted over flame until they glisten, ground by hand, and steeped in a clay jebena. The pour is shared three rounds — abol, tona, baraka — a blessing in every cup. We brought that ceremony, and the market that surrounds it, to our neighborhood.
