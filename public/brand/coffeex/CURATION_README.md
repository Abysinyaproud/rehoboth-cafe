## Curation Notes
- Quality over quantity was applied: blurry/duplicate compositions were not exported into every category.
- The folder does not contain true Ethiopian farmer, live ceremony, customer, market shelf, pastry, or shopping-experience photography. Those should still be captured later for launch authenticity.
- Several strong coffee/product images were reused across Hero, Coffee, Details, Gallery and Backgrounds because the source folder is compact and highly product-focused.
- No important subjects were cropped; images were resized by long edge only.
