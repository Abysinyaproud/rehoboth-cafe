# REHOBOTH ETHIOPIAN COFFEE

## Phase 5: Real Brand Content System

Purpose: Create the photography, video, testimonial, review, and social content needed to make the website feel specific, alive, local, and emotionally credible.

Creative direction: cinematic, editorial, natural light, warm hospitality, authentic Ethiopian coffee culture, premium through restraint. The content should feel observed, not staged. Avoid generic cafe imagery, exaggerated luxury styling, fake laughter, and stock-photo compositions.

## 3. Homepage Hero Video Plan

### Goal

Make visitors feel: “This is not just a coffee shop. This is an experience. I should visit.”

### Runtime

Ideal loop: 18-28 seconds.

### Format

- Desktop: 16:9 or 21:9 crop.
- Mobile: dedicated 9:16 crop.
- No dialogue required.
- Muted autoplay.
- Poster image required.
- Keep file compressed for performance.

### Sequence

1. Exterior arrival, 2 seconds.
2. Beans being poured or ground, 3 seconds.
3. Espresso extraction, 3 seconds.
4. Pour-over bloom, 3 seconds.
5. Coffee ceremony smoke / jebena, 4 seconds.
6. Guests receiving coffee, 4 seconds.
7. Market shelf detail, 3 seconds.
8. Founder or barista hospitality moment, 3 seconds.
9. Finished cup on table, 2 seconds.

### Visual Treatment

- Warm natural light.
- Slightly underexposed highlights.
- No aggressive color grading.
- Soft movement, tripod or slow handheld.
- Avoid fast cuts.
- Avoid smiling-at-camera scenes.

### Required Deliverables

- `hero-home-desktop.mp4`
- `hero-home-mobile.mp4`
- `hero-home-poster.jpg`
- 5 still frames for fallback sections.

## 9. Testimonial Collection System

### Testimonial Types Needed

1. Coffee quality testimonial.
2. Visit/atmosphere testimonial.
3. Ceremony experience testimonial.
4. Market/gift testimonial.
5. Remote work/local regular testimonial.

### Collection Methods

- In-store QR code leading to a short testimonial form.
- Post-event follow-up text or email.
- Receipt insert for coffee purchases.
- Founder/staff direct ask after positive interaction.
- Instagram DM follow-up after customer tags Rehoboth.

### Testimonial Form Questions

- What brought you to Rehoboth?
- What did you order or experience?
- What stood out?
- Who would you bring here?
- May we use your quote on our website/social channels?
- Name preference: full name, first name, initials, anonymous.

### Website Quote Format

- 1 short headline quote.
- 1 sentence context.
- Customer name or descriptor.

Example:

“It felt like discovering a place I wanted to bring someone back to.”

First-time guest, Columbia MD

## 10. Google Review Growth System

### Goal

Increase local search trust and convert “near me” discovery into visits.

### Review Request Moments

- After ceremony.
- After tasting event.
- After a customer compliments the coffee.
- After repeat customer purchase.
- After gift box purchase.
- After private/corporate event.

### Staff Script

“I’m so glad you enjoyed it. Reviews really help people discover us when they’re looking for Ethiopian coffee in Maryland. If you have a moment, would you mind sharing what you liked on Google?”

### Assets Needed

- Table card with QR code.
- Receipt card with QR code.
- Small sign near checkout.
- Post-event email review link.
- Instagram story “review us” slide.

### Review Response System

Respond to every review within 72 hours.

Response structure:

- Thank them by name if available.
- Mention the specific product or experience.
- Invite them back for coffee, ceremony, market, or events.

## Final Content Standard

The content should make Rehoboth feel real before the visitor arrives:

- You can sense the coffee.
- You can understand the origin.
- You can imagine the ceremony.
- You can see yourself visiting.
- You can trust that the market is curated.
- You can feel the founder behind the room.

The visual goal is not luxury for its own sake. It is hospitality made visible.
