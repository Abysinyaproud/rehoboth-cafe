# REHOBOTH ETHIOPIAN COFFEE

## Content Entry Templates

Use these templates when entering content into Sanity and Shopify.

---

## Homepage Entry

Required fields:

- Hero headline.
- Hero subheadline.
- Desktop hero video.
- Mobile hero video.
- Hero poster image.
- Origin story copy.
- Featured coffee product references.
- Ceremony gallery.
- Market gallery.
- Testimonials.
- Visit CTA.

Copy checklist:

- Does the copy make coffee the hero?
- Does it mention Ethiopia with specificity and respect?
- Does it create a reason to visit soon?
- Does it avoid generic cafe language?

---

## Coffee Page Entry

Required fields:

- Page hero image.
- Coffee category intro.
- Product references.
- Origin education copy.
- Brew guidance.
- Subscription explanation.
- Gift box copy.
- Coffee quality testimonials.

Product content:

- Name.
- Shopify handle.
- Category.
- Origin.
- Region.
- Process.
- Roast level.
- Tasting notes.
- Brew recommendation.
- Price.
- Hero image.
- Gallery.

---

## Market Page Entry

Required fields:

- Market hero image.
- Category gallery.
- Imported goods copy.
- Honey copy.
- Spices copy.
- Traditional products copy.
- Gift box content.
- In-store availability notes.
- Market testimonials.

Tone:

Curated, useful, warm, giftable. Avoid grocery-store phrasing.

---

## About Page Entry

Required fields:

- Founder name.
- Founder portrait.
- Founder short bio.
- Founder pull quote.
- Why Ethiopian coffee.
- Hospitality note.
- Founder gallery.
- Community/founder testimonials.

Interview prompts:

- Why Ethiopian coffee?
- What should first-time visitors feel?
- What does ceremony mean to you?
- Why Columbia/Maryland?
- What should people take home?

---

## Visit Page Entry

Required fields:

- Address.
- Phone.
- Hours.
- Holiday hours policy.
- Parking notes.
- Directions URL.
- Google Review URL.
- Visit hero image.
- Visit gallery.
- Accessibility notes.
- Local testimonials.

Conversion checklist:

- Can a mobile visitor get directions in one tap?
- Can a visitor call in one tap?
- Are hours clear?
- Is parking clear?
- Is the page emotionally persuasive, not only practical?

---

## Event Entry

Required fields:

- Event title.
- Slug.
- Summary.
- Start/end date.
- Hero image.
- Event gallery.
- Event video if available.
- Capacity.
- CTA label.
- CTA URL.
- Event testimonials.

Schema checklist:

- Date/time is accurate.
- Location is accurate.
- Event status is current.
- Ticket/RSVP link works.

---

## Testimonial Entry

Required fields:

- Quote.
- Source type.
- Customer name or descriptor.
- Context.
- Permission granted.
- Rating if from review.
- Portrait or customer moment if available.

Approval checklist:

- Permission is recorded.
- Quote is not over-edited.
- Source context is accurate.
- No private information is exposed.

---

## Shopify Product Entry

Required fields:

- Product title.
- Handle.
- Description.
- Price.
- Variants.
- Inventory.
- Weight.
- Shipping settings.
- Product images.
- SEO title/description.

Coffee-specific fields mirrored in Sanity:

- Origin.
- Region.
- Process.
- Roast level.
- Tasting notes.
- Brew recommendation.
- Subscription eligibility.

---

## Image Entry Checklist

For every image:

- Alt text.
- Crop/hotspot.
- Usage location.
- Orientation.
- Photographer credit if required.
- File compressed.
- No private customer data visible.

---

## Video Entry Checklist

For every video:

- Desktop and mobile crops if used in hero.
- Poster image.
- Duration under spec.
- Muted background export.
- Captions/transcript if dialogue is used.
- Compressed file.
- Safe crop reviewed.
